# Final-v4 contribution and action review

Scope: the immutable `second-draft` TSX/CSS for all five concepts. Artifacts were read before the review skill; no author receipts, earlier drafts, live source, or browser were inspected. Findings below establish source behavior; visual ambiguity is explicitly an inference.

1. **IterationOne.tsx:53,65 — selecting a sample note reproduces its entire already-visible body.** The hero cards render `note.text` in full; the reader renders the same `current.text` in full. For the four supplied short notes, opening a card adds related-note context but no new note content. This is a concrete content duplication, rather than a complaint about small text or card styling. **Minimal correction:** give the cards a purposeful preview/summary and reserve the complete body for the reader; retain the reader's related-note context and metadata.

2. **IterationThree.tsx:11,29,46 — the Work category uses the same directional symbol family as the row's open action.** Work notes receive the decorative mark `↗`; every unselected row also ends with `ArrowUpRight` to open that note. Both appear within one button, with the written category already present. **Source fact:** two arrow marks serve different meanings in the same action. **Inference requiring rendered confirmation:** the left mark can read as a second action/destination cue rather than a category motif. **Minimal correction:** give Work a clearly non-directional motif, or omit that category mark; keep the actual open indicator. This finding does not generalize to the Life star or Ideas question mark.

3. **IterationOne.tsx:32,73 and IterationFour.tsx:26,34 — saving removes the only visible explanation of temporary storage.** One shows the visit/reload limit only through `status || ...`; a successful capture replaces it with “Kept in your notebook.” Four similarly replaces “Saves are local to this page and reset when you leave” with a Saved confirmation after bookmarking. Both implementations use component state, with no persistence in these artifacts. **Consequence:** the limitation disappears precisely when a user has entrusted content or a bookmark to the demo. **Minimal correction:** keep the short storage limitation as static metadata beside a separate live status. Retain the confirmation, reset controls, and local demo behavior. Two and Five already retain their storage limitation independently of action feedback.

4. **IterationFour.tsx:23–24,34 — a related-note action can silently leave the active reader outside the chosen filter/search.** Reproduction from source: choose Reading, open Attention, then follow “A slower kind of weekend”; the filter stays Reading and its result stays Attention, while the reader becomes the Life note. `active` is independent of `results`, and the related button changes only `selected`. This is valid as an independent reading context, but that independence is not named, so the visible filter/list and active reader cease to describe one coherent selection. **Minimal correction:** either reveal the linked note in the list by adjusting the filter/search, or explicitly identify the reader as an open note retained outside the current results. Do not remove the useful related-note access. This is an action-coherence risk, not a claim that keeping a reader open during filtering is inherently wrong.
