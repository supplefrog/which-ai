The edit separates immediate command/input response from how content visibly changes. Neither an animation quota nor slide-before-zoom preference is introduced.

Parent scoped it to deliberate evaluation of this guidance at instruction-authoring. This review establishes coherent instruction boundaries only. Fresh generation and rendered judgment are required to assess behavior. Shared model/context can correlate errors; reviewer freshness does not prove independence or universal quality.
