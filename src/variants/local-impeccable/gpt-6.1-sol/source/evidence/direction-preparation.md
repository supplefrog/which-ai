# Direction preparation

Mode: Persuade. The visitor should understand a second-brain note-taking offer, see the mechanism demonstrated, and have a visible action.

Mechanism hypothesis: fragments become retrievable context through deliberate capture and connections. Audience scene hypothesis: a person returning to accumulated thoughts in daylight at a desk, between reading and project work. Cultural home: the personal systems used to turn scattered observations into something usable.

Category rut: centered SaaS promise above an app screenshot, feature cards below. Predictable opposite: a decorative brain/network cloud with barely any usable note content. Literal second-brain metaphor is also excluded from the candidate list.

Ordered grounded candidates, spanning graphic, spatial, physical, and interface traditions:

1. Research field station: numbered observation sheets, a specimen ledger, and a working index. It makes fragments legible through evidence and cross-references.
2. Transit interchange: destination hierarchy, interchange routes, and arrivals. It shows one thought becoming a path to another without decorative neural imagery.
3. Book-production proof desk: galley annotations, crop marks, revision records, and marginal notes. It makes thinking iterative and authored.
4. Signal-control console: a capture queue becoming named channels and a recalled signal. It dramatizes retrieval at a useful scale.
5. Museum study collection: catalog accession labels and grouped artifacts. It presents the value of keeping and revisiting material rather than constant production.
6. Cartographic route atlas: scale, landmarks, route keys, and indexed destinations. It makes finding a thought a spatial task with a clear legend.
7. Music rehearsal score: phrases, cue marks, and passages that return with variation. It treats linked ideas as sequences and recurring motifs.

No final direction or build path is selected here. Candidate hypotheses are authored from the common prompt; no sibling variant, shared synthesis, or personal aesthetic material was read.
