Status: user approved the rendered trial and publication after a preserved fresh draft and two bounded repair passes. ## Replacements and additions

- `SKILL.md`: replaces the open-ended dimension inventory and repeated default warning with product-led composition and contribution-based editing. Separates consistency within an established product from requested alternative concepts. Makes the design-judgment reference part of new-direction work.
- `design-judgment.md`: replaces the pre-styling decision checklist with coupled composition, type, color/material, imagery and behavior. Expands concept comparison across the full page, including the demonstration. Adds direct object interaction, shared connector geometry, purposeful asset integration and focused rendered critique.
- `motion-design.md`: replaces generic purpose and popover-only origin guidance with concept-related movement, causal origins and grouped transitions. Adds perceptible feedback and inspection of intermediate crop, overlap and competing transforms. No motion quota or obligatory entrance effect.
- `taste-selection.md`: replaces a blanket prohibition on generating comparison assets with scoped use under existing authorization; distinguishes palette samples from concept exploration.
- `personal-design-defaults.md`: makes existing native generation permission actionable even when the backend version cannot be verified. Version uncertainty must still be reported; paid/key-based fallback and publication remain outside that permission.

The other eight bundle files are unchanged. Native validation also required moving the inherited version, author and compatibility fields under `metadata`, retaining their values. No new support file or aesthetic prescription was added. Accessibility, reduced motion, functionality, licensing and tool authorization, existing identity preservation, user collaboration and the rendered review/commit/push boundary remain intact.

## Expected mechanisms and uncertainty

The previous run repeated one visible demo even though its instructions already warned about several cosmetic defaults. This revision changes the composition decision and reuse boundary rather than extending a blacklist. Alternative concepts should remain different at first glance and across their full pages; low-level behavior can still be shared. Stronger visual explanation and direct interaction should reduce compensating captions and make the product idea tangible. Coupled surface and asset decisions should produce more coherent material relationships without imposing a palette, gradients, outlines or realistic imitation. Explicit native permission removes an avoidable asset-route hesitation without requiring generation.

The bounded research supports causal motion origins, grouped transitions and deliberate framing; it does not prove more animation improves a design. More explicit guidance could still constrain a capable model or fail to change its defaults. The actual comparable rendered trial and the user's judgment decide whether this candidate improves the output. Source checks establish staging readiness only.

Evidence: [rendered output review](../../docs/frontend-output-review.md), [motion review](../../output/review-motion.md), [bounded research](research.md). Task-specific treatments and acceptance criteria remain outside reusable guidance.
